The problem

The information already exists. It's just scattered across department pages, student government sites, transit authorities, libraries, and brand programs — each maintained separately, for a different audience, with no shared index.

So you have to already know a benefit exists in order to find it. That's how universities are organized, not anyone's failure. It still means most of what you're entitled to goes unused.

How we keep it current

Every listing shows the date we last verified it, and links to its source. Time-limited offers drop off the site automatically the day they expire.

A discount that quietly ended two years ago is worse than no listing at all — so we'd rather show you a smaller catalog we can stand behind. Found something out of date? Tell us.

Who's behind it

Betterson is built and maintained by a small team of UCLA Anderson students, for the students coming after us.

Every listing links to its source — worth a click before you count on it.
