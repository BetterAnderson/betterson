The problem

The information already exists. It's just scattered across department pages, student government sites, transit authorities, libraries, and brand programs, with each maintained separately and no shared index. Most students only find out about what they have access to through word of mouth, if they're lucky enough to ask the right person.

Which means the benefits you actually use depend more on who you happen to talk to than what you qualify for.

How we keep it current

Every listing shows the date we last verified it, and links to its source. Time-limited offers drop off the site automatically the day they expire.

A discount that quietly ended two years ago is worse than no listing at all — so we'd rather show you a smaller catalog we can stand behind. Found something out of date? Tell us.
